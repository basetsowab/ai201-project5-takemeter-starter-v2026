# Your label definitions, one per line, as `label: definition`.

concept: asks how or why a financial product, rule or market mechanism works in general, and the answer would be the same for anyone who asked
procedure: asks how to carry out a specific step, or what a rule, form or fee says applies, where a correct answer exists in the rules or the institution's terms
decision: asks which option the poster should choose for their own situation, where the answer depends on their goals, risk or circumstances rather than on a rule
